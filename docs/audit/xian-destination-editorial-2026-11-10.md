# Xi’an destination editorial refinement

Local implementation: 2026-11-10. No commit, push or deployment.

## Content and product scope

- Recommend two full sightseeing days, usually three nights when arrival and departure
  are separate travel days; distinguish elapsed nights from usable sightseeing time.
- Place the Terracotta Warriors outside the center as the main visit of one day, with
  the Old City, City Wall and Muslim Quarter on another.
- Compare three practical hotel areas and include transfer, noise and evening tradeoffs.
- Clarify public museum entry, identity-linked admission, crowds, walking and ticket
  limits; private guiding does not imply exclusive access.
- Match three real products to distinct needs: Xi’an-only day tour, six-day Xi’an–Beijing
  route, and ten-day Beijing–Xi’an–Shanghai route.
- Link contextual guidance for museum timing, accessibility and Beijing/Xi’an trip length.
  Distinguish admission included in the day tour from paid additions and station/airport
  connections, which are separate. Product information checked against the actual tour
  source, rather than the catalog's abbreviated note.
- Replace abstract cultural and food descriptions with Qin/Tang chronology, museum
  interpretation, local dishes and specific dietary questions.
