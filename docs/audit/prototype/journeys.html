How it works

  1. i

    Tell us about your trip

    Dates, who is travelling and what matters most. A few lines is enough.

  2. ii

    Receive a written proposal

    Route, hotels, inclusions and price, in writing, normally within a day.

  3. iii

    Adjust until it is right

    Change the days, the pace or a hotel. Nothing is booked until you confirm.

  4. iv

    Travel with your own team

    A private guide and driver, and our China team on WhatsApp throughout.

Good questions to ask

Are your journeys private or group tours?

Private. Your guide, driver and vehicle are for your party alone.

Can I change a published journey?

Yes. Each is a starting point. Dates, pace, hotels and days can be adjusted before you confirm.

How are journeys priced?

Individually, for your dates and group, in a written proposal before you pay.

What if nothing here fits?

Tell us what you have in mind and we will design it from a blank page.

Tell us when, and who is coming.

A China-based specialist replies on WhatsApp, normally within a day. No payment, no obligation.
