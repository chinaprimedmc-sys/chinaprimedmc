## Editorial and conversion scope

- Explain two full sightseeing days / usually three nights, with travel-day logic and
  optional extra-day interests rather than an overfilled checklist.
- Compare the Bund/central Huangpu, former-concession/Jing’an addresses and Lujiazui;
  explain exact hotel location, road access, room noise and walking tradeoffs.
- Distinguish Pudong Airport (PVG), Hongqiao Airport (SHA), its terminals, Hongqiao Railway
  Station and the large Pudong district. Do not infer airport proximity from a hotel name.
- Match the actual day-tour inclusions: private guide and vehicle, central-hotel service,
  Yu Garden interior admission, one ordinary shared public-ferry crossing. Meals, tower,
  sightseeing cruise, extra hours and airport/port/station service are separate unless
  confirmed. No special-access, ship-return or current visa eligibility claim is made.
- Compare three published products by fit: Shanghai city day, ten-day capitals route,
  eight-day Shanghai–Zhangjiajie route. Do not imply flights or hotels beyond product terms.
- Add six visible FAQs, descriptive article links, food details and respectful residential
  neighborhood guidance. Existing FAQ schema continues to use the same visible answers.
