# Shanghai search intent and editorial ownership

Local intent map, 10 November 2026. This organizes existing content and candidate
long-tail queries. No current SERP review, keyword difficulty, search volume or ranking
was measured. Priorities reflect relevance to a real planning decision and service fit.

| Intent / candidate query                                               | Primary owner                                              | Destination-page treatment                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

The destination is a decision page. Explain enough for a visitor to choose a base,
length of stay and service, then link to the specialist owner rather than duplicating
its complete checklist. A private city day is distinct from a layover or shore excursion;
an ordinary ferry is distinct from a sightseeing cruise.

After publication, review Search Console query/page pairs, product clicks and qualified
enquiries. Overlapping impressions alone do not prove harmful cannibalization. Verify
current search results before describing any candidate phrase as low competition.
