Keep the destination's answer concise, visible and consistent with product boundaries.
A private guide does not replace park buses, and a second hotel day does not automatically
include park re-entry. Avoid duplicating the transport, altitude or seasonal articles.

After publication, review Search Console query/page pairs, product clicks and qualified
enquiries. Multiple landing pages with impressions do not alone establish harmful
cannibalization; use intent and performance to decide whether consolidation is needed.
