# Chengdu search intent and editorial ownership

Local intent map, 10 November 2026. Priorities reflect relevance to a travel decision
and AVIORA's published services. Current SERPs, keyword volume, difficulty and ranking
were not measured. None of these phrases is claimed to be low competition.

| Intent / candidate query                                | Primary owner                                         | Destination-page treatment                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chengdu private tour planning; where to stay in Chengdu | `/destinations/chengdu`                               | Main intent: base, nights, panda/city balance and the right product.                                 |
| How many days in Chengdu                                | `/journal/how-many-days-in-chengdu-itinerary`         | Give a concise nights-versus-full-days answer and link to deeper planning.                           |
| Chengdu Panda Base morning visit                        | `/journal/chengdu-panda-base-morning-visit-itinerary` | Explain the early-start logic, named facility and gate; article owns detailed visit planning.        |
| Chengdu Panda Base tickets foreigners                   | `/journal/chengdu-panda-base-tickets-foreigners`      | State identity-linked entry; article owns detailed booking preparation and current-rule checks.      |
| Chengdu panda visit heat / rain / accessibility         | `/journal/chengdu-panda-base-heat-rain-accessibility` | Give realistic walking and animal-viewing limitations, then link to specialist advice.               |
| Chengdu panda day tour early morning                    | `/tours/private-chengdu-panda-day-tour-early-morning` | Product owns price, pickup, admission, confirmed hours and optional extras.                          |
| Five-day Chengdu panda food private tour                | `/tours/chengdu-pandas-sichuan-table`                 | Product owns four-night structure, services and the optional Leshan day.                             |
| Five-day Chengdu Leshan itinerary                       | `/journal/5-day-chengdu-leshan-itinerary`             | Destination compares city-only and extension choices; article owns day-by-day research.              |
| Chengdu Jiuzhaigou seven-day private tour               | `/tours/chengdu-pandas-jiuzhaigou-private-7-day-tour` | Product owns the published three-plus-three-night route and ending in the Jiuzhaigou area.           |
| Chengdu to Jiuzhaigou transport                         | `/journal/chengdu-to-jiuzhaigou-transport`            | Destination identifies it as a separate regional transfer; transport article owns options.           |
| Panda Base with children / ethical panda visit          | Existing family panda guides                          | Give brief practical limitations; do not duplicate family checklists or imply animal-contact access. |
| Chengdu Sichuan food without very spicy dishes          | `/destinations/chengdu` as a supporting theme         | Explain dish choices, numbing vs heat and specific dietary questions; avoid generic promises.        |

Use the destination to help choose a stay and product, and specialist articles to answer
detailed questions. Do not turn the page into a second ticketing or day-by-day article.
After publication, review Search Console query/page pairs, product clicks and qualified
enquiries. Overlapping impressions alone do not prove harmful cannibalization; check
intent and performance before changing ownership or consolidating pages.
