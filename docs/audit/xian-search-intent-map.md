is not measured keyword research. Search volume, current SERP competition and ranking
were not verified. Priorities reflect service fit and query specificity.

## Use and measurement

Use one primary landing page for each informational or transactional intent. Contextual
links should answer the visitor's next question. Do not duplicate the specialist itinerary
or product inclusions on the destination page. Check Search Console query/page pairs and
qualified enquiries after publication; overlapping impressions by themselves do not prove
harmful cannibalization. Reassess competition using current search results before assigning
effort by difficulty.
