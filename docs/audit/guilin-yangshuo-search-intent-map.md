## Priorities and measurement

Prioritize itinerary and cruise-finish questions because they signal a specific
planning decision and match published AVIORA content. Keep destination prose
useful without reproducing the logistics articles. Link from the exact decision
paragraph to the detailed guide; link to the product where private coordination
solves the stated problem. Do not imply that the public vessel is private, that
accommodation is included, or that optional activities always operate.

After publication, inspect Search Console query/page pairs for destination,
article and product URLs. Check relevance, impressions and qualified enquiries
before consolidating pages; overlapping impressions alone do not prove harmful
cannibalization. No low-competition claim is made without current SERP research.
