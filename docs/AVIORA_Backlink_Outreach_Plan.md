## Outreach Rules

- Send one personalized message per publication or organization.
- Do not attach files in the first message; offer approved materials instead.
- Do not claim that TTG Asia, MATTA or any publication endorses AVIORA.
- Ask for a link only where it is factually relevant to the reader.
- Follow up once after seven days, then stop unless the recipient replies.
- Record whether each link is editorial, sponsored, nofollow or not published.
- Do not pay for a link without checking the publication, disclosure and link attributes first.
