# AVIORA Content and SEO Standards

## Audience and Positioning

Write for international mid-to-high-end travelers who value expert judgment, reliable private service and clear planning. Show quality through selected hotels and room categories, exceptional guides, private transfers, sensible routing, written confirmations and responsive local support. Do not rely on repeated claims of luxury.

## Page Hierarchy

- H1: 4-9 words and immediately understandable to someone unfamiliar with China.
- Hero description: 18-24 words, focused on the route and strongest service proof.
- Card summary: 18-30 words. It must remain readable at every supported viewport.
- SEO paragraph: 45-70 useful words placed below the main conversion content.
- CTA: one clear action supported by a specific description of what happens next.

## Voice

- Use concise, restrained and specific English.
- Prefer concrete nouns and decisions: hotel location, room category, guide, vehicle, transfer, ticket, timing and local support.
- Avoid excessive use of `luxury`, `private`, `journey`, `seamless`, `curated`, `beautifully paced` and similar abstract language.
- Never publish unverifiable superlatives, customer numbers or awards.
- State dietary, mobility and Muslim-friendly support as planning capabilities, not guarantees, until suppliers are confirmed in writing.

## Search Metadata

- Title: 45-60 characters where practical; lead with destination, duration or search intent and append the brand only once.
- Description: 140-160 characters where practical; identify route, private service and a useful differentiator.
- Use one canonical URL, one indexable H1 and unique copy for every indexable page.
- Unknown detail URLs must return HTTP 404, not a 200 response with a Not Found message.
- Add internal links to relevant destinations, tours, planning guidance and one conversion page.

## New Tour Checklist

1. Use a direct English title with duration and destinations or defining experience.
2. Add a short hero statement, route, duration, suitable traveler types and service style.
3. State the recommended hotel level and room assumptions without making unconfirmed promises.
4. Provide a day-by-day itinerary with one clear focus and image per day where available.
5. Separate included, excluded and optional services.
6. Add confirmed group-size pricing or leave explicit price placeholders; never invent prices.
7. Add practical notes for dietary, mobility, family or Muslim-friendly requests where relevant.
8. Write a unique SEO title, description, FAQ and structured data.
9. Verify card title wrapping, hero crop and CTA placement on mobile, tablet and desktop.
10. Run lint, typecheck, production build and the SEO audit before deployment.
