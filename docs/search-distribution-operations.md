# Search and AI Distribution Operations

This is the operational checklist for AVIORA China Travel. It does not claim that any external account has been verified, claimed, or configured.

## Before submitting

- Confirm that the legal operator name, Guangzhou registered address, WhatsApp number, website, and social profiles match the published Company page.
- Use the guest-facing name AVIORA China Travel for consumer search profiles. Use China Prime DMC only for the travel-trade brand context described on the Company page.
- Do not create a public office listing for Beijing, Shanghai, or Xi'an unless that office is staffed and eligible under the applicable platform address policy.

## Google and Bing

1. Verify the site in Google Search Console using the configured verification method.
2. Submit `https://www.chinaprimedmc.com/sitemap.xml` in Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
3. Inspect the home page, Company page, Reviews page, a representative journey, and a representative journal article after material releases.
4. Monitor indexing, canonical selection, soft 404 reports, crawl errors, rich-result reports, and the queries that reach commercial pages.
5. Enable IndexNow only after an actual key and submission workflow are configured. Do not represent it as enabled before then.
6. Claim a Google Business Profile only for a real, policy-compliant business presence. Keep its name, contact details, website, and address consistent with the Company page.

## Monthly record

- Indexed journal articles and journeys.
- Non-brand organic impressions and clicks.
- Impressions, clicks, and average position for priority commercial queries.
- Journal-to-journey click-through rate.
- Journey-to-inquiry conversion rate.
- Brand-query coverage for AVIORA, AVIORA Travel, AVIORA China Travel, and China Prime DMC.
- A small documented sample of AI answers: whether the brand, operator relationship, services, and evidence boundaries are represented accurately.

## Quarterly content review

Prioritize visa, entry, ticketing, rail, airport, payment, weather, accessibility, and price pages. For each page, check the linked primary source, update the visible facts where necessary, update the fact-check date and structured `dateModified`, and allow the sitemap to reflect that actual edit.

When consolidating a weak or duplicative article, choose one canonical owner, add a permanent redirect from the retired URL, and update internal links before release. Do not keep pages merely to increase article count.

## Release checks

- Run the journal governance, editorial, search-strategy, quality, journey-link, structured-data, SEO, type, and production-build checks.
- Verify `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/llms.txt`, and `/llms-full.txt` return current, mutually consistent information.
- Validate representative structured data in Schema.org Validator and Google Rich Results Test. Do not add aggregate-rating or review rich-result markup unless the data and policy qualify independently.
