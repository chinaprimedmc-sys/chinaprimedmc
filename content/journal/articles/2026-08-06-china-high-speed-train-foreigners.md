# China High-Speed Rail, Clearly

## Draft

Foreign visitors can buy real-name China Railway tickets with a valid passport through official channels, including the 12306 English website or app. The passport used for purchase is also the journey credential: carry the original to enter the station, pass identity checks and board. Allow time for security, large-station navigation and any manual passport verification.

High-speed rail is often the strongest city-centre-to-city-centre choice on routes such as Beijing–Xi’an or Shanghai–nearby Yangtze River Delta cities. It is not automatically better than flying on every sector. Compare total door-to-door time, departure station, seat availability, luggage and the day’s wider itinerary.

## Book through a traceable channel

China Railway states that 12306 is its official customer-service website and that the railway department does not handle problems arising on other websites. A travel company or booking platform can still be useful, but the traveler should understand who issued the ticket, how changes are handled and where the confirmed train number appears.

For every passenger, enter the name, passport type and passport number exactly. A mismatch can create verification problems at the station. If a passport is renewed after booking, do not assume the old ticket will work with the new document; resolve the credential change before travel.

## E-tickets and the passport journey

On an e-ticket route, the booking is electronic. China Railway says passengers should retain the e-ticket information and present the valid ID document used for purchase to check in, exit and board. An itinerary sheet or reimbursement receipt is not itself a ticket.

Keep the booking confirmation offline with:

- train number and travel date;
- departure and arrival station names;
- scheduled departure time;
- carriage and seat number;
- passenger passport details; and
- booking-channel support contact.

Chinese cities often have several stations with similar names. “Beijing,” “Beijing South” and “Beijing West” are not interchangeable; neither are Shanghai’s principal stations. Put the exact station in the day plan and calculate the transfer from the hotel to that entrance.

## How early to reach the station

There is no responsible universal promise such as “20 minutes is always enough.” China Railway notes that ticketing, security, ID verification, gates and platforms are separated by walking time, that queues may form, and that ticket sales and checks stop before departure according to station announcements.

For a first journey, AVIORA plans a conservative station arrival based on the city, station, party size, mobility, holiday pressure and whether a guide or assistant is accompanying the transfer. Summer holidays and major public-holiday periods deserve additional margin.

### What happens inside

1. Enter the correct station access point.
2. Present the passport if directed for identity verification.
3. Put all baggage through mandatory security screening.
4. Find the waiting area and exact ticket gate on the displays.
5. Watch the gate opening time and platform assignment.
6. Board the carriage shown on the ticket and use the assigned seat.

Some gates read foreign passports directly; another journey may require staff assistance. Treat a manual lane as normal operational variation, not a ticket failure.

## Choosing a seat class

Train type determines the available seating. On many high-speed services, second class provides a practical, comfortable base; first class offers a wider seat and a quieter, lower-density cabin; business class is the most spacious and carries a significant price premium. Exact layouts and services vary by train set, so confirm the specific service rather than promise a universal configuration.

Choose around the traveler:

- **Second class:** good value for most independent adults and families comfortable with a busier cabin.
- **First class:** useful when more personal space and an easier working or resting environment justify the difference.
- **Business class:** worth considering for long sectors, privacy priorities or travelers for whom space materially changes comfort.

Book the party in one order where practical, but never guarantee adjacent seats until the ticket assignment is confirmed.

## Baggage and accessibility

China Railway’s English FAQ states a free carry-on limit of 20 kg for most passengers, with each item up to 20 kg. It lists a maximum combined dimension of 130 cm for EMU trains. Foldable wheelchairs used by travelers with disabilities are excluded from that range.

This rule is not an invitation to bring the maximum. Large suitcases must still be moved through security, station concourses and carriage storage. A mid-to-high-end rail plan works better with manageable luggage, a vehicle sized for it and porter expectations confirmed rather than assumed.

Some large stations provide priority and wheelchair services, but the FAQ advises consulting the station for details. Request mobility support before the journey and design transfers around walking distance, not only train duration. AVIORA’s [senior-friendly planning page](/senior-travel) explains why these loads must be designed.

## Changes, missed trains and operational risk

China Railway has formal endorsement, refund and destination-change rules, all dependent on timing, ticket availability and ticket status. Do not publish a blanket claim that every ticket is freely changeable. If plans move, contact the issuing channel immediately.

A missed train can consume the day’s availability during busy periods. Protect the sector by avoiding a tight same-day connection from an international flight and by keeping the guide, driver and hotel informed through one contact path.

## FAQ

### Can foreigners book China train tickets online?

Yes. China Railway’s 12306 English website accepts valid foreign passports under its stated rules and offers ticket purchase, change and refund services.

### Is a paper ticket required?

Not for an e-ticket journey. Carry the original passport used for booking and retain the electronic booking information. An itinerary sheet is not a boarding credential.

### How much luggage can I take on a high-speed train?

The official FAQ lists 20 kg for most passengers, a 20 kg maximum for each item and a 130 cm combined-dimension limit on EMU trains. Reconfirm before travel and keep luggage practically manageable.

### Is first class necessary?

No. Second class works well for many travelers. First or business class can be worthwhile when space, cabin density or a long sector materially affects comfort.
