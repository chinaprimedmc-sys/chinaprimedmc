## Draft

If shopping is not part of the trip you want, ask for that preference in writing before you book. A clear no-forced-shopping policy means required sightseeing does not depend on visiting a store, and travelers are not expected to make a purchase to keep the day moving.

AVIORA states that its private journeys do not include forced shopping. Travelers may still choose to visit a market, craft studio or shop when it genuinely matches their interests; the distinction is choice. The route should be designed around the experiences agreed with the traveler, not around a purchase expectation.

## What “no forced shopping” should mean in practice

The phrase is most useful when the operator can answer practical questions:

- Are any retail visits built into the required sightseeing day?
- Can we decline a shop without losing a planned attraction or transfer?
- Are guides or other suppliers expected to sell products?
- If we want to visit a market or craft workshop, is it optional and how is it described?
- Can the itinerary state that shopping is not a required stop?

These questions clarify the experience without assuming that every shop, market or workshop is inappropriate. A visit can be meaningful when it is chosen, explained and connected to the traveler’s interests.

## Shopping is different from a cultural encounter

A working craft studio, food market or family-run business can offer genuine context. The traveler should know who they are meeting, what the visit involves, whether anything is being sold and whether a purchase is expected. “Local experience” is not enough detail to distinguish an encounter from a retail stop.

Ask the operator to describe the activity in plain language. If the main purpose is to see how something is made or to speak with a craftsperson, that should be clear. If the stop includes a showroom or optional sales, say so before the day.

## Why the policy matters to the route

Time is limited. An unplanned stop can displace a neighborhood walk, a longer conversation at a heritage site or an earlier return for a family. A private itinerary should make the use of the day visible and leave the traveler in control of optional choices.

This is one reason a written daily sequence matters. ## How AVIORA approaches it

AVIORA’s private journeys are planned around the traveler’s priorities, with no forced shopping. The team can include a market, design shop or craft visit when the traveler asks for it and when the activity fits the route. It should remain an informed choice, not a condition of receiving the promised sightseeing service.

Before travel, the written itinerary is the best place to record the preference. Travelers can say what they enjoy, what they would rather skip and whether they want time for independent browsing. The operator can then plan the day accordingly and clarify any optional purchase or admission costs.

## Questions to ask before paying

1. Can you confirm in writing that no shopping visit is required?
2. Does declining an optional shop affect the guide service or planned sightseeing?
3. Are any craft or market visits included, and what happens there?
4. Are purchases, tastings or workshop fees separate from the tour price?
5. Can the itinerary show where free time is available?

Compare the answers rather than relying on a label alone. Also review the complete [inclusions guide](/journal/what-is-included-private-china-tour), because meals, tickets and activities should not be assumed from descriptive copy.

## Questions travelers ask

### Does no forced shopping mean there is no shopping at all?

No. It means shopping is optional. A traveler can still choose a market, store or craft visit that interests them.

### Should a shopping-free policy be included in the contract?

Ask for it in the itinerary or written service description so the expectation is clear to both sides. Review the contract terms for the controlling language.

### Are markets and craft studios always sales stops?

No. They vary. Ask what the visit involves, who hosts it, whether a purchase is expected and whether the stop can be skipped.

### Can we ask the guide to avoid shopping during the trip?

Tell the operator before travel so the preference can be shared with the local team. A guide can then keep the day focused on the agreed plan.
