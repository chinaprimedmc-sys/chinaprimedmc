# Terracotta Army Tickets, Clearly

> [!summary] Editorial brief
> **Audience:** Foreign passport holders planning a first visit to the Terracotta Army from Xi’an  
> **Search intent:** Reserve, enter and pace the museum correctly  
> **Primary keyword:** Terracotta Army tickets for foreigners  
> **Business relevance:** Supports AVIORA’s flagship Beijing–Xi’an–Shanghai route with reliable passport and transfer planning

## Draft

Foreign visitors must reserve the Terracotta Army in advance through an accepted real-name booking channel and bring the **original passport used for the reservation**. The official Qin Shi Huang Mausoleum Museum page says all visitors, including foreign visitors and people eligible for free admission, must book ahead. At entry, the visitor, ticket and identity document must match.

The museum is in Lintong District, outside central Xi’an. Treat it as a dedicated half-day excursion with a confirmed vehicle, ticket time and return plan—not as a quick stop between the railway station and a flight.

## The official booking rules that matter

The attraction’s formal name is the **Emperor Qinshihuang’s Mausoleum Site Museum** or **Qin Shi Huang Mausoleum Museum**. Its official visitor page describes a real-name online reservation system using the official website and official WeChat accounts.

For a foreign passport holder, five details need to agree:

1. full name as shown in the passport;
2. passport number;
3. nationality and any other required identity fields;
4. selected visit date and time slot; and
5. the original passport presented at the gate.

Do not substitute a passport photo, hotel copy or a different travel document unless the museum’s current instructions expressly allow it. If a passport has been renewed after booking, contact the booking channel before travel rather than assuming the old number will be accepted.

## What is included in the museum visit

The official page states that the Qin Shi Huang Mausoleum Museum consists of two parts:

- the **Terracotta Army Museum**, where the excavated pits and associated displays are located; and
- the **Qin Shi Huang Mausoleum Archaeological Site Park**, also called Lishan Garden.

The same-day ticket includes shuttle service between the two sites according to the current official visitor information. The page advises allowing approximately 1.5 hours for the museum components, but a private itinerary should protect more time for security, orientation, shuttle queues, interpretation and the party’s walking pace. A half-day block is a planning recommendation, not an official minimum.

## Current opening and ticket information

As verified on August 8, 2026, the official page lists:

- **Peak season, March 16–November 15:** ticket checking from 8:30, last check at 17:00.
- **Low season, November 16–March 15:** ticket checking from 8:30, last check at 16:30.
- **Standard adult admission:** RMB 120, with the same listed price in peak and low seasons.

Major-holiday opening hours are subject to the latest announcement. Prices, discount eligibility and operating times can change, so the live museum page remains the authority at booking and again shortly before the visit.

The museum currently instructs time-slot visitors to arrive about 30 minutes before their reserved time. It also states that the North Gate is the entrance and that the visitor, ticket and identity document must match at verification.

## A booking sequence for foreign visitors

### 1. Decide the Xi’an date before buying the ticket

The museum day should not compete with a same-day long-distance train or international flight. Confirm the Xi’an hotel nights and intercity transport first. AVIORA’s [nine-day Beijing, Xi’an and Shanghai route](/tours/first-china-beautifully-paced) gives the Terracotta Army its own day rather than treating it as a transfer stop.

### 2. Use the museum’s current official instructions

Start at the museum’s official visitor page and follow its current booking channel. A marketplace may be convenient, but it must still produce a valid real-name reservation. Check what the price includes, the cancellation terms and where support is provided if the passport entry fails.

### 3. Enter every passport carefully

Copy the machine-readable identity details exactly. For a family or private group, check every traveler separately; one correct adult booking does not repair an error in another passenger’s record.

### 4. Save proof in more than one format

Keep the order record, booking reference, selected time and customer-service contact offline. Carry the original passports in a secure but reachable place. A guide can help interpret the process, but cannot override a mismatch between the reservation and the identity document.

### 5. Reconfirm holiday notices

During National Day, school holidays or other demand peaks, capacity and hours may be adjusted. Check the museum’s latest notice shortly before travel. If no ticket is secured, do not travel to Lintong expecting a guaranteed walk-in solution.

## How to pace the pits

Pit 1 provides the largest visual scale, but starting with the biggest hall is not always the best interpretive sequence. A guide may begin with the discovery story, construction method and smaller displays before using Pit 1 as the main reveal. The exact order should follow live gallery access and visitor flow.

Inside the halls, expect long standing periods, dense viewing rails and changing light. Photographers should follow current museum rules and never use flash where prohibited. Families may need a shorter interpretation plan; older travelers may need rest points and a less ambitious second site.

The useful goal is not to see every label. It is to understand the relationship between the army, the First Emperor’s mausoleum complex, ancient production systems and the archaeological work that continues to shape the site.

## Transfer planning from Xi’an

“Xi’an to the Terracotta Army” is not a single fixed travel time. The result depends on hotel district, time of day, traffic, the chosen entrance and any road controls. A private transfer should state:

- hotel pickup time and Chinese address;
- museum reservation time;
- exact entrance and meeting point;
- driver and guide contact details;
- whether Lishan Garden is included in the plan; and
- the latest sensible departure for the next confirmed event.

If Xi’an is reached by high-speed rail, verify the station name and passport booking using the [China high-speed train guide](/journal/china-high-speed-train-foreigners). Avoid placing the museum immediately after a train whose delay would erase the entry buffer.

## FAQ

### Can foreigners book Terracotta Army tickets with a passport?

Yes. The official museum page includes passports among accepted identity documents and states that foreign visitors must use the real-name advance reservation system.

### Do I need the original passport at the Terracotta Army?

Yes. The official instructions require the original valid identity document used for the booking, and the visitor, ticket and document must match.

### Can I buy Terracotta Army tickets at the gate?

Do not rely on walk-in sales. The museum says all visitors must reserve in advance through the official website or official WeChat channels.

### How early should I arrive for my time slot?

The current official guidance tells time-slot visitors to arrive about 30 minutes before the reserved time.

### Is Lishan Garden included?

The current official visitor page says the ticket covers both the Terracotta Army Museum and the archaeological site park, with shuttle service between them. Confirm the live terms when booking.
