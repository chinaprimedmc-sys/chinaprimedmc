## Draft

AVIORA's published 12-day family journey currently starts from **US$6,880 per person**, or **US$27,520 for a family of four** on the stated basis: two adults and two children aged 6–11 sharing two rooms, outside Chinese public holidays, school-holiday pressure dates and other peak periods.

That number is useful only with its assumptions attached. A private family journey is not priced like four identical seats on a group departure. Rooms, child ages, vehicle and luggage capacity, guide allocation, domestic transport and private experiences all shape the total.

The relevant product is AVIORA's [12-day private China family tour with pandas](/tours/china-family-tour-with-pandas-12-day-private-tour), covering Beijing, Xi'an, Chengdu and Shanghai.

## What the published starting price includes

The current published basis includes:

- Eleven nights in selected premium five-star family hotels
- Two rooms for the family on the stated occupancy basis
- Private family-ready English-speaking guides on confirmed touring days
- Private vehicles sized for the family and normal luggage
- Listed entrance tickets
- Beijing-to-Xi'an and Xi'an-to-Chengdu first-class high-speed rail
- A nonstop Chengdu-to-Shanghai economy-class domestic flight
- Selected meals and the listed hands-on private experiences
- China-based family journey support

International flights, visas, travel insurance, personal spending, unlisted meals and optional upgrades are not automatically included unless the proposal says otherwise.

## Cost driver 1: room configuration

Two standard rooms, adjoining rooms, confirmed connecting rooms and a family suite are different products. Hotels may accept a connecting-room request without guaranteeing it until close to arrival, or may have very limited inventory.

The proposal should name the exact room categories and show whether the relationship between rooms is confirmed, requested or illustrative. If the family requires a guaranteed internal connecting door, that requirement can narrow the hotel list and increase the price.

## Cost driver 2: the children's ages

Children's ages affect hotel occupancy, bedding, breakfast, attraction tickets and transport fares. A child who can share existing bedding may be priced differently from a teenager requiring a full bed or separate room. Supplier rules do not follow one universal international definition of “child.”

China Railway currently applies published age-based child-ticket rules, but the exact fare and document requirement should be checked when the ticket is booked. Hotels and attractions can use different thresholds.

## Cost driver 3: travel dates

Chinese public holidays, summer pressure, international school breaks and major local events can affect hotel and transport availability. Golden Week is not simply a small seasonal supplement; crowding and inventory can change the route's quality.

If the dates are fixed during a high-pressure period, the operator should identify what needs earlier confirmation and whether the same budget would produce a better experience on different dates.

## Cost driver 4: private service scope

Private guide and vehicle cost is shared across the party. Four travelers may have a more efficient per-person basis than two. But the family also needs the correct vehicle for passengers, luggage and any child-seat request.

The quotation should specify guide days, vehicle days, airport and station transfers, service hours and overtime. “Private throughout” is not a price basis unless the scope is defined.

## Cost driver 5: domestic movement

This route uses two first-class rail sectors and one domestic flight. Prices can change with class, schedule and availability. The full cost is not only the ticket: it includes both station or airport transfers, luggage assumptions and the effect on the day's usable time.

A cheaper flight at an awkward hour may cost the family more in lost sleep and disrupted meals. Premium planning evaluates the complete day.

## Cost driver 6: experiences and specialists

Private tai chi, a family photographer, clay-warrior making, a private kitchen or an archaeology specialist should have a defined host, duration and purpose. Availability-dependent specialists should be labeled accordingly.

The question is not how many upgrades can be added. It is which experiences make the route more meaningful for the children's actual ages and interests.

## What the final proposal should state

The final written proposal should show the family total and per-person equivalent, traveler ages, room count, bedding, breakfast, rail class, flight baggage, included meals, guide and vehicle coverage, ticket status, exclusions, payment schedule and cancellation terms.

A high price does not prove quality. A low price does not prove value. The useful comparison is whether the complete family operation is visible before payment.

## FAQ

### Is US$27,520 the final price for every family of four?

No. It is the published starting total on the stated basis. Dates, children's ages, exact rooms, hotels, transport and selected experiences determine the final written quote.

### Are international flights included?

No, not in the published land price. AVIORA can design the land route around confirmed international flights.

### Can we use one room for four people?

Only when the named hotel and room category permit that occupancy and it suits the family. The published product assumes two rooms.

### Why can a private family tour cost more per person for three travelers?

Guide, vehicle and some transfer costs are shared across fewer travelers, while room and transport needs may not reduce proportionally.
