## Draft

For a multi-city China trip, pack so each traveler can move one main case through hotel lobbies, station security and train boarding without depending on a porter. Then write down who handles every bag at each handoff. The main question is not whether a train or aircraft technically accepts the luggage. It is whether the travelers can move the luggage through the complete day.

High-speed rail is convenient, but passengers normally remain responsible for their own bags. Domestic flights have airline- and fare-specific checked and cabin baggage rules. Private vehicles have finite luggage space. A route that works with two medium cases may not work with four large cases, a stroller and several carry-ons.

## Use the one-main-case test

The simplest planning standard is one manageable main case per traveler, plus one small personal bag. Families can distribute differently, but the group should still be able to move together without one adult pulling three cases while supervising children.

Before travel, test the packed case over a short walk and lift it to the height of a car trunk. If that is difficult at home, it will be more difficult after a long flight, in a busy station or on a rainy platform.

This is not a minimalist packing contest. Medical equipment, baby supplies and specialist gear may be essential. Those items should be disclosed so the vehicle, station assistance and room storage can be planned around them.

## Trains: the published allowance is not the whole answer

China Railway publishes passenger baggage rules and states that luggage must not obstruct movement or public areas. Storage varies by train type and carriage. Smaller cases may fit above the seats; larger cases commonly use designated areas near carriage ends or other available spaces. Space is not assigned like a seat.

Arrive early enough to place bags calmly. Keep passports, medicine, valuables and the next-hotel information in a personal bag at the seat. Do not pack an item needed during the journey at the bottom of a large case.

If the group has many bags, first-class rail does not automatically create enough luggage space. The useful question is whether the selected train, boarding station and group can handle the bags. Read the [China high-speed train guide](/journal/china-high-speed-train-foreigners) before finalizing the transfer.

## Domestic flights: check the actual airline and fare

Domestic baggage allowances vary by airline, cabin, route, aircraft and fare product. Check the operating carrier's official conditions for the booked ticket, including weight, size, checked allowance, excess charges and restricted items. A ticket sold through another airline or platform may still be operated under the carrier's rules.

Power banks and spare lithium batteries require particular attention. They generally belong in cabin baggage, not checked luggage, and China has current marking and capacity requirements for domestic aviation. Review the [China domestic flight power bank guide](/journal/china-domestic-flight-power-bank-rules) close to departure.

## Hotel-to-hotel transfer map

For every city change, list the chain:

1. Room to lobby.
2. Lobby to vehicle.
3. Vehicle to station or airport entrance.
4. Security and check-in or identity control.
5. Waiting area to train or gate.
6. Arrival platform or baggage claim to exit.
7. Exit to the correct meeting point.
8. Vehicle to the next hotel.
9. Lobby to room.

Then assign responsibility. A driver may load ordinary bags into the vehicle but may not enter the station. A guide may assist through the station but cannot carry every case. Hotel bell service may be available, but it should not be the only way the group can move.

## Families, older travelers and mobility needs

For families, one adult should remain responsible for the children during boarding while another supervises luggage. A compact stroller and fewer loose bags make that division possible. For older travelers or anyone with balance or strength concerns, reduce the number of pieces and arrange assistance where it is actually available.

Do not disguise a mobility issue as a luggage issue. If lifting, distance or standing is difficult, tell the planner directly. The [China walking guide](/journal/how-much-walking-china-tour) helps describe the wider physical load, while the [family rail guide](/journal/china-high-speed-trains-with-children) focuses on children and boarding.

## A practical packing system

- Keep one packing cube or small bag ready for each one- or two-night stop.
- Put medication, documents and one change of clothes in cabin baggage.
- Use luggage identifiers that are easy to describe.
- Save a photo of every case before departure.
- Leave room for purchases instead of adding a new case halfway through.
- Pack a small foldable bag only if the return flight allowance supports it.
- Keep the next hotel's Chinese address and booking reference offline.

Laundry can reduce volume on longer journeys. Confirm service timing before relying on same-day return. A hotel change every one or two nights makes laundry and repacking harder; that is another reason to avoid an overloaded route.

## How luggage changes the itinerary

A large luggage plan can make rail less attractive for a short sector, require a larger vehicle or turn a tight flight connection into a poor choice. It can also make a same-day attraction after arrival unrealistic. AVIORA compares the transfer as a complete day, not just the published travel time.

The [12-day Shanghai, Chengdu, Xi'an and Beijing journey](/tours/beijing-xian-chengdu-shanghai-private-11-day-tour) contains three intercity connections. The luggage plan helps determine departure timing, vehicle size and whether the next experience belongs on the arrival day.

## Questions travelers ask

### How much luggage can I take on a China high-speed train?

China Railway publishes baggage conditions, but practical storage also depends on the train and available space. Verify current official rules and pack so your party can board and store the bags without blocking aisles.

### Are there porters at Chinese railway stations?

Assistance availability varies and should not be assumed. Plan to manage your own bags unless a specific service has been confirmed for the station and time.

### Should I ship luggage between hotels?

Hotel-to-hotel shipping may be possible through commercial services, but timing, tracking, prohibited items and hotel acceptance must be confirmed. Do not ship passports, medication, valuables or items needed immediately.

### What vehicle size do we need?

The answer depends on passenger count and the exact dimensions and number of bags. Send the operator a realistic luggage list before the vehicle is booked.

### Can the guide carry our bags?

A guide may help with an ordinary handoff, but guiding is not a porter service. Arrange appropriate assistance for substantial luggage or mobility needs.
