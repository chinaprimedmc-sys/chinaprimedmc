## Draft

Your first 24 hours in China should do three things: **get you legally and physically settled, make tomorrow easy, and prove that the people operating the trip are connected to one another.** A premium arrival is not a theatrical airport greeting. It is a sequence in which the driver has the correct terminal, the hotel has the correct names, the room request is understood, the traveler can reach support, and the first morning does not begin with missing information.

Keep your passport, phone, arrival address, payment backup and operator contact accessible until hotel check-in is complete. Do not place the only copy of a required document in checked luggage. If someone is meeting you, know the exact meeting point and what to do if the flight, immigration or baggage claim takes longer than expected.

| Handoff         | Traveler should know                                 | Operator should have checked                       |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Before landing  | Entry basis, arrival address, contact method         | Flight, terminal, traveler names, delay monitoring |
| Airport meeting | Exact point, sign or identification, fallback number | Driver dispatch, vehicle fit, luggage capacity     |
| Hotel arrival   | Property name, room basis, passport registration     | Reservation name, room request, late arrival note  |
| First briefing  | Tomorrow's start, weather, dress, walking, documents | Guide assignment, tickets, vehicle and backup plan |
| First night     | How to reach support and what must charge overnight  | Any unresolved issue has an owner and deadline     |

## Before the aircraft door opens

### Keep the arrival essentials together

Use one small document wallet or secure phone note for:

- Passport and any required entry document
- International and domestic flight details
- First hotel name, address and telephone number
- Operator's China contact and emergency channel
- Airport meeting instructions
- Travel insurance details
- Essential medication in original labeled packaging
- A payment backup separate from the primary card or phone wallet

The address should be available in English and, when possible, Chinese. That is useful even with a private transfer because an immigration officer, airport staff member or backup driver may need it.

### Know what your operator is monitoring

A private transfer plan should use the actual flight number and terminal, not only an estimated arrival time. Ask whether the dispatcher monitors flight changes and how long the included waiting period is after landing. International arrivals can vary because of gate position, immigration, baggage delivery and customs.

If the flight diverts or the baggage is delayed, contact the operator before leaving the agreed meeting process. The response should be operational: a revised meeting point, driver update or clear instruction, not simply “please wait.”

## Arrival formalities: what the travel company can and cannot do

Your operator can prepare information, check the route, provide the first hotel address and coordinate pickup. It cannot decide whether a traveler is admitted. Entry eligibility and the completeness of documents remain the traveler's responsibility and are determined by the relevant authorities.

Do not rely on a marketing page or an old screenshot for entry rules. Check the applicable Chinese embassy, consulate or National Immigration Administration information for your nationality, passport, full itinerary and travel date. Transit-without-visa arrangements have specific conditions; a private tour booking does not override them.

Once admitted, keep the passport accessible. It may be needed for hotel registration, train travel and passport-linked attraction reservations during the trip.

## The airport pickup should be designed for failure as well as success

### A complete meeting instruction contains five details

1. Terminal and arrival hall
2. Meeting point after customs
3. How the representative will identify themselves
4. A China-reachable telephone or messaging contact
5. What to do after a stated waiting time if the parties do not see each other

“Your driver will be waiting” is not enough in a large international airport. A meeting point should be specific enough to survive a crowded hall and a phone that has not connected to local data.

### The vehicle should fit people and luggage

The proposal may have priced a vehicle by passenger count, but luggage volume matters on arrival day. Tell the operator about large hard-shell cases, mobility equipment, strollers, sports equipment and unusually heavy hand luggage. A technically legal seat count does not guarantee a comfortable transfer.

The driver and guide may be separate people. If no guide meets the flight, know whether the driver speaks English and how the operations team will communicate if needed. That can be perfectly workable when it is planned and explained.

### Build a communication fallback

International roaming, airport Wi-Fi, eSIM activation and app logins can all fail at the exact moment they are needed. Save the contact in more than one form. A screenshot of the meeting instruction and a standard phone number can be more useful than a link inside an app that has not loaded.

## Hotel arrival and accommodation registration

Hotels in China register foreign guests using their valid travel documents and submit the relevant accommodation information to public security authorities. Present the passport used for the reservation and allow time for the front desk to complete the process. The English name sequence in one system may not look identical to another, so the booking name should be checked before arrival.

For stays in a private residence or another non-hotel accommodation, the foreign guest or host has separate registration obligations. The National Immigration Administration states that registration is required within 24 hours of arrival. On September 21, 2026, it rolled out nationwide online registration through official NIA channels while keeping local in-person registration available. Confirm the current process for the actual address.

### Before leaving the front desk, check the room facts

- Correct room category and number of nights
- Bedding configuration
- Breakfast location and service time
- Deposit or incidental hold
- Connecting or nearby-room request
- Wi-Fi access
- Departure or next-day meeting point
- Early breakfast or packed breakfast, if a departure requires it

If the room is wrong, report it immediately to both the hotel and the operating contact. A quiet issue log is more useful than waiting until checkout: what is wrong, who owns it, and by when will it be resolved?

## Set up payments without making one app a single point of failure

Many travelers use Alipay or Weixin Pay in China, and international bank cards may be linked where supported. Cash and physical cards remain useful backups because app verification, network access, merchant acceptance and bank security controls can vary.

During the first 24 hours:

- Confirm the payment app opens on mobile data or hotel Wi-Fi
- Test a small purchase before relying on it for an important transaction
- Keep some RMB cash for simple contingencies
- Carry a physical bank card separately
- Know how your bank handles overseas verification
- Never give a guide or driver your payment password or verification code

A private trip may include most major services, but travelers still need a practical way to buy personal meals, drinks, souvenirs or independent transport.

## The first briefing should reduce tomorrow's cognitive load

The arrival briefing does not need to be long. After an overnight flight, quality means selecting the information that matters now.

Confirm:

- Guide name and meeting time
- Exact lobby or entrance meeting point
- Weather and appropriate clothing
- Expected walking, stairs and standing
- Which passport or ticket documents to carry
- Meal plan and dietary notes
- Return estimate and evening plan
- Whether the vehicle stays with the party
- Contact method if someone needs to stop early

If the first full day includes the Forbidden City, Great Wall, a train or another time-sensitive reservation, the guide or operations team should confirm the relevant document and start time before the traveler goes to sleep.

## If a train journey comes next

China Railway operates real-name ticketing. Foreign passengers use valid passport information to purchase eligible tickets and should carry the same document used for the booking. The official 12306 guidance also advises allowing enough time for security, identity checks, station navigation and boarding controls.

Before the first train sector, receive:

- Departure station in English and Chinese
- Train number and departure time
- Seat or berth class
- Pickup time from the hotel
- Who accompanies the party into the station
- The passport details used for the booking
- What to do if the passport has changed or is lost
- Arrival pickup or onward transfer plan

Do not assume “Beijing Station” means any station in Beijing. Major cities have multiple stations, and a wrong-station transfer can consume the margin intended for security and boarding.

## The first day should not try to repay the airfare

Long-haul travelers often want to start immediately because the trip feels valuable. The better question is whether the first activity improves orientation or borrows energy from the first important day.

A good arrival-day activity is flexible, close to the hotel, easy to leave and not dependent on a scarce timed ticket. A short neighborhood walk or unhurried meal can help reset the body. A cross-city transfer followed by a major heritage site usually creates pressure without improving the experience.

For families, older travelers and anyone crossing many time zones, protect water, food and sleep before adding “just one more stop.” Private travel should make that choice easier, not make travelers feel guilty for using the flexibility they purchased.

## What a reliable operator resolves before the first night

- Missing or incorrect hotel reservation details
- Room or bedding mismatch, or a documented resolution plan
- Unclear next-day guide and vehicle assignment
- Ticket or passport-detail discrepancy
- Airport baggage issue that affects onward travel
- Dietary note not reaching the first included meal
- Uncertainty about a station, terminal or pickup point
- No working way to reach the traveler or operating team

Some supplier issues cannot be solved instantly. Reliability is visible in ownership: one person acknowledges the issue, states the next action and gives a time for the next update.

## A first-night readiness check

Before sleeping, verify:

- My passport is secure and accessible.
- My phone and power bank are charging.
- I can reach the operating contact.
- I know tomorrow's meeting time and place.
- I know what to wear and carry.
- Any dietary, walking or room issue has been acknowledged.
- My next transport sector matches my passport details.
- I know which services tomorrow are included.
- I have one payment backup.

If those nine points are clear, the trip has moved from arrival mode into journey mode.

## FAQ

### Will a private driver meet me inside baggage claim?

Usually the meeting is in the public arrivals area after immigration, baggage claim and customs because drivers and representatives generally cannot enter restricted baggage areas. Your instructions should name the exact public meeting point.

### Does the hotel register my stay in China?

Hotels register foreign guests using valid travel documents and submit the required accommodation information. For private residences or other non-hotel stays, the guest or host has separate registration responsibilities and should confirm the current local process.

### Should I hand my passport to the guide?

You may need to present it for hotel, rail or attraction procedures, but you should understand why it is needed and retain control of the document whenever practical. Do not leave it with an unidentified person or use an insecure channel to send passport data.

### What if I cannot find my airport driver?

Stay at the agreed meeting point, check the written fallback instruction and contact the operator using the saved phone or messaging details. Do not accept an unsolicited ride from someone who cannot verify the booking.

### What should I do on my first evening in China?

Choose a flexible meal or short walk near the hotel, test connectivity and payment, confirm the next morning and sleep. Avoid making a scarce, high-effort attraction the success test for an arrival day after a long flight.
