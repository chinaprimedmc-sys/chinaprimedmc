# Guilin & Yangshuo — 5 nights / 6 days

**One-line summary:** Karst peaks, river light and village roads, with enough time to let the landscape settle rather than seeing it through a boat window and a coach window.

## Research summary (Step 1)

**Why this is a proven Western leisure route:** Guilin–Yangshuo is a recurring standalone module in China Highlights, WildChina, Audley, Intrepid, G Adventures and TourRadar. The Li River cruise, Yangshuo countryside and a flexible day for villages or cycling recur across listings and reviews; the route is sold as an accessible contrast to China’s large cities.

**Common duration:** 4–6 nights.

**Typical sequence:**

1. Arrive Guilin; Reed Flute Cave or city lakes.
2. Li River cruise Guilin to Yangshuo; West Street evening.
3. Yangshuo countryside by bike, e-bike or vehicle; cooking or cormorant-fishing add-on.
4. Longji Rice Terraces day trip (often optional, overnight variant).
5. Transfer back to Guilin or onward by high-speed rail.

**Usually included:** Private transfers, Li River tickets, English-speaking guide, boutique or riverside hotels, breakfast and selected meals. Standard products often use shared cruise boats, fixed cycling loops and a compressed Longji day.

**Typical audience:** Families, photographers, first-time visitors, soft-adventure travellers and seniors when the terrace walk is moderated.

**Working source pattern:** The route is a named China Highlights/Audley/WildChina staple; Intrepid/G Adventures group itineraries repeat the river + Yangshuo countryside pairing; TourRadar review patterns focus on scenery, guide organisation and the trade-off between crowds and early starts.

## Comparative analysis (Step 2)

**Typical version:** A shared Li River cruise followed by a fixed coach-and-bike circuit. “English-speaking guide” is the main quality signal; the day can become crowded, wet and physically repetitive. Longji is frequently added as an ambitious full-day excursion with little recovery time.

**Our version:** We choose the best river departure for the season and your mobility, then meet you at the landing with a private car rather than funneling into a coach. In Yangshuo, the guide can swap cycling for a village walk, short electric-cart ride or a shaded café. Longji is a considered option—not an achievement badge—with an overnight or gentle viewpoint plan for seniors and young children.

## Client-facing copy (Step 3)

### The route

The Li River is famous for its peaks, but fame is not the same as atmosphere. The difference is time: a quiet bend of water, a village road taken because it looks interesting, a guide who knows when to stop talking so the landscape can do its work. This is southern China at its most restorative.

### Day by day

**Day 1 — Guilin, lakes and a soft landing**  
Your driver meets you and takes you to a calm Guilin base. Depending on arrival time, we might walk by the city lakes or visit Reed Flute Cave; or we simply have dinner and recover from the journey.

**Day 2 — Downriver to Yangshuo**  
We arrange the Li River cruise around the day’s conditions and your preferred level of activity. From the boat, the karst landscape changes almost imperceptibly. At Yangshuo, your private car avoids the usual transfer shuffle and brings you to your hotel for an evening by the river.

**Day 3 — The countryside at your speed**  
A local guide leads a private route through rice fields and village lanes. You may cycle, ride an e-bike, walk a short stretch or remain in the car between viewpoints. A cooking class or a quiet lunch with a view gives the day shape without turning it into an endurance test.

**Day 4 — A free-form Yangshuo day**  
Leave room for weather and mood: a bamboo-free alternative if the river is high, a painter’s village, a morning market or simply a late breakfast. Your guide remains available, but the day is intentionally breathable.

**Day 5 — Longji, if the terraces call**  
Those who want the layered rice terraces can make the private journey north, stopping at accessible viewpoints and taking the walk in sections. Others can stay in Yangshuo or return to Guilin early. We do not make a difficult climb the price of admission.

**Day 6 — Departure**  
Your driver takes you to Guilin station or airport, with time for one last look at the limestone horizon.

### Who it suits

Families looking for visual wonder without constant museum logistics; older travellers who value scenic access over strenuous trails; and first-time visitors wanting a calm counterpoint to Beijing and Shanghai.

### Practical notes

April–May and September–October generally offer the most comfortable combination of water levels and temperatures. Cruise decks and village paths can be slippery. Yangshuo’s best experiences are weather-sensitive, so we keep the order adjustable.
