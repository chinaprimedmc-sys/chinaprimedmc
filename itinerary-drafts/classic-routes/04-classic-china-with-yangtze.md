# China at an Easier Pace — Beijing, Xi’an, the Yangtze & Shanghai

**One-line summary:** A 14-day, 13-night China classic for older travellers and multi-generation groups, using a Three Gorges cruise to reduce hotel changes without losing cultural depth.

## Research summary (Step 1)

### 市场地位与证据

北京—西安—长江三峡—上海是传统欧美中国全景团的重要结构。Viking、Victoria Cruises、Wendy Wu、AmaWaterways、Sanctuary式高端产品、China Highlights及TourRadar长期把三峡游轮嵌入Golden Triangle或北京—西安—重庆—宜昌—上海路线。主要购买理由是少换酒店、内陆景观和适合成熟客群。

### 常见时长与顺序

- 常见时长：13–17天。
- 北京3–4晚 → 西安2晚 → 重庆1晚/登船 → 3–4晚游轮 → 宜昌 → 上海2–3晚。
- 岸上常见：丰都/白帝城/小三峡/三峡大坝，实际停靠随船期改变。

### 市场通常包含

游轮舱房、船上餐、团体岸上游览、国内交通和陆地段导游。即便陆地段是私人，岸上活动通常仍是统一时间、统一巴士和统一步速。

### 常见营销对象

60+旅客、退休夫妇、多代家庭、不喜欢频繁换酒店者，以及希望以较低物流压力看中国内陆的首次访华者。

## Comparative analysis (Step 2)

### Typical version

船上舒适，但岸上仍可能早起、排队和长距离行走。不同舱房位置、升降机距离、登船坡道和岸上台阶经常在购买前解释不足。陆地段为了配合船期被压缩。

### Our version

- 北京和西安仍使用私人车辆与私人导游，不因加入游轮而压缩核心文化日。
- 订船前核对船龄、舱房位置、阳台、升降机、餐饮和英文服务，而不是只写“豪华游轮”。
- 岸上项目逐个说明真实体力；可以跳过一次团体游览，在船上休息。
- 重庆登船和宜昌离船均由真人协调，处理行李、码头变化和私人接送。
- 上海保留两晚，避免游轮结束后立刻衔接国际航班。

## Client-facing copy (Step 3)

### Overview

The Yangtze changes the logistics of a China journey: for several days, the landscape moves while your room stays still. This route keeps Beijing and Xi’an private, uses the cruise for restful distance, and finishes in Shanghai with enough margin to feel restored rather than processed.

### Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive in Beijing**  
Private airport transfer and no compulsory sightseeing.

**Day 2 — Imperial Beijing**  
Give the Forbidden City the morning, with a route adjusted around walking comfort.

**Day 3 — Temple and neighbourhood life**  
Visit the Temple of Heaven, then choose a lighter cultural afternoon.

**Day 4 — The Great Wall**  
Travel privately to a suitable Wall section and use cable-car support where appropriate.

**Day 5 — High-speed rail to Xi’an**  
Station and luggage coordination make the journey easier. Settle in without a packed evening.

**Day 6 — The Terracotta Army**  
Explore the pits with a guide who can shorten the museum route when standing becomes tiring.

**Day 7 — Fly to Chongqing and embark**  
Private transfers handle both airports and the changing river-port logistics. Board, unpack and let the room become home for the next few nights.

**Days 8–10 — The Three Gorges**  
Wake to a different river each morning. Attend a lecture, stay on deck or join selected shore excursions. We explain which outings involve slopes, stairs or long transfers, and skipping one is always acceptable.

**Day 11 — Yichang and onward to Shanghai**  
Disembark with luggage support. Visit the Three Gorges Dam only if it adds value, then continue by the most sensible rail or air connection.

**Day 12 — A slow Shanghai morning**  
Recover from the travel day. A neighbourhood lunch or riverfront walk is enough.

**Day 13 — Shanghai in full**  
Connect the Bund, lived-in streets, food and contemporary architecture in one private day.

**Day 14 — Departure**  
Private airport transfer timed around your confirmed flight.

### Who this route suits

Older travellers, multi-generation groups, first-time visitors who dislike packing every two nights, and couples who want scenery with a stable daily base.

### Practical notes

Ship schedules and shore calls change. Gangways may be steep and some docks use steps. Cabin selection matters for noise and lift access. Spring and autumn are comfortable; summer is hot and humid. The cruise is shared, while land arrangements and transfers remain private.
