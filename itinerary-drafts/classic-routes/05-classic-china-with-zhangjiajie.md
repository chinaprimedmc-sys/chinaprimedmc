# China’s Dramatic Landscape — Beijing, Xi’an, Zhangjiajie & Shanghai

**One-line summary:** A 12-day, 11-night first-China route pairing the country’s essential historic cities with Zhangjiajie’s sandstone pillars and cloud-filled mountain views.

## Research summary (Step 1)

### 市场地位与证据

Zhangjiajie通过“Avatar Mountains”获得稳定Western awareness。China Highlights、WildChina、Odynovo、Wendy Wu、TourRadar及大量Viator/GetYourGuide产品长期把它与北京、西安、上海组合。常见产品核心是袁家界、天子山、天门山和玻璃桥，但评论中反复出现排队、台阶、天气和体力问题。

### 常见时长与顺序

- 常见完整线：11–14天。
- 北京3–4晚 → 西安2晚 → 张家界3晚 → 上海2–3晚。
- 张家界段通常为袁家界/天子山一天、天门山一天、玻璃桥或宝峰湖半天。

### 市场通常包含

国内航班、景区门票、共享景区接驳、缆车和英文导游。私人团也无法替代景区内部公共系统，但可以改善入口、顺序和外部接送。

### 常见营销对象

自然景观爱好者、摄影旅客、带较大孩子的家庭、希望在首次访华线路中加入“与城市完全不同”体验的人。

## Comparative analysis (Step 2)

### Typical version

以完成全部网红项目为目标，玻璃桥、天门洞、袁家界连续出现。天气不好时仍按原顺序执行；导游只负责带路，较少解释哪个平台值得排队。长者替代方案不清楚。

### Our version

- 张家界保留3个夜晚和天气机动空间，不把一次云雾遮挡视为行程失败。
- 导游根据能见度、排队和膝盖情况决定入口、缆车方向与观景平台。
- 玻璃桥完全可选；宝峰湖、较低强度观景台或休息日都是完整替代方案。
- 私人司机解决机场、酒店和景区入口间的复杂转移。
- 家庭成员可以短时分组，再由导游和司机协调会合。

## Client-facing copy (Step 3)

### Overview

Zhangjiajie is often sold through superlatives. Its real effect is quieter: stone pillars appearing through cloud, a cable car crossing a forested void, a view that changes while you stand still. This route places that landscape between China’s historic opening and its modern finale, with enough flexibility for mountain weather.

### Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive in Beijing**  
Private arrival and rest.

**Day 2 — The Forbidden City**  
Explore the palace axis at a human pace, with a flexible afternoon.

**Day 3 — Temple of Heaven and local Beijing**  
See the city’s ceremonial and everyday layers without filling every hour.

**Day 4 — The Great Wall**  
Choose a Wall route according to fitness, weather and crowd conditions.

**Day 5 — High-speed rail to Xi’an**  
Travel with station and luggage support, then settle in.

**Day 6 — The Terracotta Army**  
Spend a focused day with the warriors and keep the evening optional.

**Day 7 — Fly to Zhangjiajie**  
Private airport transfers lead to a calm base near Wulingyuan.

**Day 8 — Yuanjiajie and Tianzi Mountain**  
Use shuttles, elevators and cable cars strategically. Your guide chooses a few strong viewpoints rather than asking the group to conquer every platform.

**Day 9 — Tianmen Mountain, by choice**  
The cable-car ascent is the centre of the day. Glass walkways, the long stairway and extended boardwalks remain optional.

**Day 10 — A weather-flex day, then Shanghai**  
Return to a view missed in cloud, visit Baofeng Lake or take a quiet morning before flying east.

**Day 11 — Shanghai’s modern rhythm**  
Move privately between the riverfront, neighbourhoods, food and architecture.

**Day 12 — Departure**  
Private airport transfer after an unhurried final morning.

### Who this route suits

Families with older children, photographers, active first-time visitors and older travellers who can manage some steps but value transparent alternatives.

### Practical notes

Mountain visibility changes quickly. Expect public shuttles, queues, stairs and uneven paths even on a private journey. April–May and September–October are generally comfortable. Travellers with severe vertigo or limited mobility should discuss Tianmen options before booking.
