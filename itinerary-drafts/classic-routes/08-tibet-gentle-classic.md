# Tibet, with Time to Acclimatise — Lhasa, Yamdrok & Shigatse

**One-line summary:** A 7-day, 6-night cultural Tibet route that begins with proper acclimatisation and stops short of treating Everest Base Camp as an obligation.

## Research summary (Step 1)

### 市场地位与证据

西藏是Audley、Wendy Wu、Explore、China Highlights、Tibet specialist operators和TourRadar长期存在的高价值中国产品。Western-facing路线通常分为拉萨短线、拉萨—日喀则文化线和珠峰大本营长线。本方案选择中间层：需求成熟，但比珠峰版更适合舒适型和成熟客群。

### 常见时长与顺序

- 常见温和版：6–8天。
- 拉萨适应与市区3–4晚 → 羊卓雍措 → 江孜 → 日喀则1–2晚 → 返回拉萨。
- 许可、导游与车辆必须提前协调；行程可能因政策、天气或道路调整。

### 市场通常包含

入藏许可协助、私人或团体车辆、当地英文导游、酒店、景点和部分餐食。市场文案经常强调神秘与壮观，却没有同等篇幅解释海拔、楼梯、供暖、氧气和医疗距离。

### 常见营销对象

有强烈文化兴趣的西方旅客、二次访华者、摄影者和身体状况良好的成熟客群。

## Comparative analysis (Step 2)

### Typical version

抵达第二天即安排布达拉宫大量台阶，随后快速前往高海拔湖泊和日喀则；客户在签约前可能只看到“moderate fitness”。珠峰常被作为默认升级，增加长车程与更高风险。

### Our version

- 抵达日和第二天都优先适应，不以“浪费时间”看待休息。
- 布达拉宫时段放在身体状态稳定后，提前说明台阶和不可控的预约规则。
- 私人车中准备水、保暖和应急用品，司机与导游持续观察状态。
- 羊卓雍措和日喀则可因天气、道路或身体反应缩短。
- 不默认销售珠峰；客户必须理解额外高度、住宿和医疗限制后单独决定。

## Client-facing copy (Step 3)

### Overview

Tibet should not be rushed into revealing itself. The first task is simply to arrive, breathe and let the body adjust. From Lhasa’s monasteries, the route moves gradually toward Yamdrok Lake and Shigatse, with a private guide and driver watching the practical conditions as closely as the landscape.

### Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive in Lhasa and do less**  
Meet your driver, check in and rest. Hydration, warmth and sleep are the only priorities.

**Day 2 — Acclimatisation and a gentle first look**  
Take a short, low-pressure walk near the hotel or through a manageable section of the old city. Return immediately if headache or fatigue grows.

**Day 3 — Lhasa’s sacred centre**  
Visit Jokhang Temple and selected old-city lanes with your guide. The sequence is kept light enough to preserve energy for the Potala Palace.

**Day 4 — The Potala Palace**  
Enter at the assigned time and take the climb slowly. The afternoon remains free for recovery.

**Day 5 — Yamdrok Lake and onward to Shigatse**  
Travel by private vehicle, stopping at viewpoints according to weather and wind. Continue through Gyantse when conditions and energy make sense.

**Day 6 — Shigatse and return to Lhasa**  
Visit Tashilhunpo Monastery with a shortened walking option, then return privately to Lhasa with breaks as needed.

**Day 7 — Departure**  
Private transfer to the airport or station. We keep departure morning unprogrammed.

### Who this route suits

Healthy adult travellers with a strong cultural interest, repeat China visitors and photographers willing to accept that the body and local conditions may change the plan.

### Practical notes

Lhasa is approximately 3,650 metres; some route points are higher. No itinerary can guarantee prevention of altitude illness. Travellers with heart, lung or significant medical conditions must seek professional medical advice. Permits and site appointments are subject to regulation. This route is not automatically suitable for every 60+ traveller.
