# China in City and Landscape — Beijing, Xi’an, Guilin, Yangshuo & Shanghai

**One-line summary:** A 13-day, 12-night first-China route that places the Li River and Yangshuo’s countryside between the country’s three essential cities.

## Research summary (Step 1)

### 市场地位与证据

北京—西安—桂林/阳朔—上海是Audley、Wendy Wu、China Highlights、WildChina、G Adventures、Intrepid和TourRadar长期出现的完整China highlights结构。Golden Triangle解决历史与城市，桂林提供一眼可懂的自然对比。Li River cruise + Yangshuo countryside几乎是所有Western-facing版本的固定组合。

### 常见时长与顺序

- 常见时长：11–15天。
- 常见顺序：北京3–4晚 → 西安2晚 → 桂林1晚 → 漓江游船 → 阳朔2晚 → 上海2–3晚。
- 部分产品增加龙脊梯田，通常作为长途一日游或桂林前一晚。

### 市场通常包含

城市间高铁/航班，Li River共享游船，阳朔自行车或电动车团体活动，英文导游、早餐和部分午餐。普通版本中的阳朔“自由日”往往实际销售多个固定加购项目。

### 常见营销对象

第一次来中国、摄影爱好者、家庭、希望在文化城市之间加入自然恢复段落的长者或情侣。

## Comparative analysis (Step 2)

### Typical version

桂林常被压成“住一晚、第二天坐船、第三天飞走”。共享游船后的码头接送拥挤；骑行被视为默认活动；龙脊梯田可能在没有解释真实车程和台阶的情况下被加入。

### Our version

- 桂林/阳朔保留3个完整夜晚，漓江不是交通工具，而是路线中段的情绪转换。
- 游船按季节、水位和客人行动能力选择；下船后私人车直接接往酒店。
- 阳朔活动可以在骑行、短步行、车辆观景、烹饪或河边休息之间切换。
- 龙脊不是“必须完成”的景点；若加入，可改成住宿一晚或低强度观景版本。
- 上海安排在最后，让国际离境更轻松，也给全程一个恢复段落。

## Client-facing copy (Step 3)

### Overview

China’s first cities tell you how the country was built; the Li River shows you how it has been imagined. Between Beijing’s palace walls, Xi’an’s buried army and Shanghai’s waterfront, Guilin and Yangshuo create a necessary pause—water, limestone and days that can change with the weather.

### Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive in Beijing**  
Private airport meeting and a quiet check-in. Rest comes first.

**Day 2 — The Forbidden City**  
Walk a selected palace route with historical context, seated pauses and an optional lighter afternoon.

**Day 3 — Temple of Heaven and Beijing texture**  
Begin with the park’s morning life, then choose hutongs, the Summer Palace or rest.

**Day 4 — The Great Wall**  
Travel privately and select the Wall section around season, mobility and crowd tolerance.

**Day 5 — High-speed rail to Xi’an**  
We manage the station transition. A gentle first evening replaces a second sightseeing schedule.

**Day 6 — The Terracotta Army**  
Let the warriors carry the day. Add Xi’an food or the city wall only if energy allows.

**Day 7 — Fly to Guilin**  
Private transfers at both ends make the southbound transition straightforward. Walk by Guilin’s lakes or simply settle in.

**Day 8 — Down the Li River to Yangshuo**  
The river carries you through changing karst horizons. On arrival, your private car avoids the usual group-transfer shuffle and takes you directly to the hotel.

**Day 9 — Yangshuo countryside, chosen by you**  
Cycle, walk, ride an electric cart or travel between viewpoints by car. A cooking experience or long countryside lunch can become the day’s centre.

**Day 10 — Weather room and a slower morning**  
Leave one day free enough to follow river conditions and mood: a village, a market, a short boat experience or no programmed activity at all.

**Day 11 — Fly to Shanghai**  
Return to Guilin airport privately and continue east. Meet the city first through the Huangpu River at dusk.

**Day 12 — Shanghai, old and new**  
Connect riverfront architecture, local streets, food and contemporary life without compulsory shopping.

**Day 13 — Departure**  
Keep the morning flexible before your private airport transfer.

### Who this route suits

First-time visitors who do not want an all-city trip; families who need outdoor variety; seniors who want scenic access without a fixed hiking requirement; photographers and couples drawn to quiet landscape.

### Practical notes

April–May and September–October are usually comfortable. River conditions and rain may change the exact order. Cruise decks and village paths can be slippery. Longji adds significant driving and walking and should be discussed separately.
