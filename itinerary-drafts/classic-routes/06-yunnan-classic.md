# Yunnan, Slowly Revealed — Kunming, Dali, Lijiang & Shangri-La

**One-line summary:** A 9-day, 8-night journey through lakes, old towns and highland culture, with Shangri-La included only when altitude and appetite for distance make sense.

## Research summary (Step 1)

### 市场地位与证据

云南是Western-facing定制中国市场中最成熟的二次访华路线之一。Audley、Wendy Wu、WildChina、China Highlights、Intrepid、G Adventures和TourRadar长期销售昆明—大理—丽江—香格里拉，或大理—丽江的温和缩短版。稳定卖点是少数民族文化、洱海、丽江古城、玉龙雪山、虎跳峡和香格里拉藏区文化。

### 常见时长与顺序

- 常见时长：8–12天。
- 昆明1晚 → 大理2晚 → 丽江2–3晚 → 香格里拉2晚。
- 城市间使用高铁和私人车辆；丽江—香格里拉可用高铁或车辆，视景观停靠与体力决定。

### 市场通常包含

精品酒店、高铁、私人或小团车辆、英文导游、古城步行、玉龙雪山缆车和部分民族村落体验。常见问题是连续换酒店、古城商业化路线、强制演出和对海拔适应解释不足。

### 常见营销对象

二次访华旅客、情侣、摄影和文化兴趣旅客、带较大孩子的家庭，以及希望看到城市之外生活方式的人。

## Comparative analysis (Step 2)

### Typical version

每天安排古城、村落、表演和购物街；大理与丽江的差异被快速打卡消解。玉龙雪山和香格里拉海拔被当作卖点，较少讨论头痛、睡眠、台阶和临时取消。

### Our version

- 大理与丽江各保留两晚以上，避免每天搬行李。
- 私人车沿洱海和丽江周边可以随时停、跳过或提前回酒店。
- 不把“民族文化”变成固定商业演出；由当地导游连接真实社区、手艺、宗教与饮食。
- 玉龙雪山根据天气、缆车状态和身体情况决定高度。
- 香格里拉是完整但可删除的章节；温和版在丽江结束，不把高原当成必修课。

## Client-facing copy (Step 3)

### Overview

Yunnan rewards travellers who are willing to leave room between destinations. A courtyard in Dali, changing light on Erhai Lake, the lanes above Lijiang and the higher, quieter world of Shangri-La each need a different rhythm. Private travel allows the route to rise gradually—and to stop rising when your body says enough.

### Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive in Kunming**  
Meet your driver and recover. A lakeside walk or local dinner is optional.

**Day 2 — Stone Forest and onward to Dali**  
Visit a selected section of the Stone Forest before taking the high-speed rail to Dali. We keep the walking route realistic and avoid filling the transfer evening.

**Day 3 — Dali and Erhai Lake**  
Move privately between lakeside viewpoints, a village and Dali’s old town. The day can favour photography, food or a long courtyard lunch.

**Day 4 — A slower Dali morning, then Lijiang**  
Leave room for market life or rest before traveling to Lijiang. Settle above or beside the old town where vehicle access and quiet nights are considered.

**Day 5 — Lijiang beyond the busiest lanes**  
Explore with a local guide who can connect Naxi history, architecture and everyday life. Choose a short walk rather than following the entire commercial circuit.

**Day 6 — Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, conditionally**  
Use private transport and the appropriate cable-car level. If weather or altitude makes the mountain unwise, replace it with a valley, village or cultural day.

**Day 7 — Toward Shangri-La**  
Travel by rail or private road, optionally pausing at an accessible Tiger Leaping Gorge viewpoint. Check in and keep the first highland evening quiet.

**Day 8 — Monastery and highland life**  
Visit Songzanlin Monastery with enough time to understand the setting and religion, then choose old-town streets or a countryside view. Stairs can be shortened.

**Day 9 — Departure**  
Fly or travel onward with private transfer support. The route can also end in Lijiang for travellers who prefer to avoid the highest section.

### Who this route suits

Repeat visitors, culturally curious couples, families with older children, photographers and active older travellers who want a slower regional journey.

### Practical notes

Kunming and Dali are moderate; Lijiang and Shangri-La rise substantially in altitude. Shangri-La is not recommended automatically for every senior. Spring and autumn are generally comfortable; summer rain can alter mountain views. Old-town stones are uneven and vehicle access is limited in some lanes.
