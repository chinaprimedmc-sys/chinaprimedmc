# Chengdu, Pandas & Sichuan Table

**One-line summary:** A 5-day, 4-night private Chengdu journey built around an early panda morning, tea-house life and Sichuan food, with Leshan offered by choice.

## Research summary (Step 1)

### 市场地位与证据

成都作为独立4–6天产品和中国长线延伸，长期出现在Wendy Wu、G Adventures、Intrepid、China Highlights、WildChina、Odynovo及TourRadar。稳定核心是熊猫基地、人民公园/茶馆、川菜和乐山。它是家庭客户最容易理解的中国目的地产品之一。

### 常见时长与顺序

- 常见时长：4–6天。
- 抵达 → 熊猫基地与市区 → 川菜/文化 → 乐山一日游 → 离开。
- 部分线路加入都江堰、青城山或熊猫志愿体验，受政策与年龄限制。

### 市场通常包含

私人或小团车辆、英文导游、熊猫门票、早餐及部分餐食。常见问题是熊猫早起后继续塞满全天、固定辣味菜单、乐山被默认加入而不解释车程和台阶。

### 常见营销对象

家庭、长者、美食爱好者、第一次访华延伸和需要较轻松节奏的旅客。

## Comparative analysis (Step 2)

### Typical version

熊猫基地只是一天的第一个项目，之后继续武侯祠、锦里、茶馆和晚餐；客户疲劳时没有独立车辆离开。导游常仅以“licensed English-speaking”描述，食物按固定团餐执行。

### Our version

- 熊猫基地是Day 2唯一大型体验，下午只保留茶与休息。
- 私人车与司机全程服务，可提前返回或临时改变。
- 英文导游经过人工筛选，重点是沟通、判断和照顾，而不仅是证件。
- 川菜按过敏、辣度和儿童接受度选择。
- 乐山为选择项，可替换成成都市场、茶馆、画廊或长午餐。
- 规划和落地阶段均可直接联系真人。

## Client-facing copy (Step 3)

### Overview

Chengdu is a city with a soft centre. Pandas may be the reason to begin, but tea, food and the unforced rhythm of local life are why the city deserves more than a stopover. Five private days leave room to enjoy the obvious highlight and still notice everything around it.

### Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive into Chengdu’s easy pace**  
Private airport or station transfer. Take a short neighbourhood walk only if the journey has left you with energy.

**Day 2 — Pandas before the day begins**  
Enter early, when the animals are most active. Your guide keeps the route manageable, then returns with you by private car for lunch and tea in People’s Park.

**Day 3 — A city understood through taste**  
Connect Wuhou Shrine or an old neighbourhood with a market, cooking session or carefully chosen Sichuan meal. Spice is adjusted to the people at the table.

**Day 4 — Leshan, or more Chengdu**  
Make the private journey to the Giant Buddha and choose the least strenuous useful viewpoint, or remain in Chengdu for a slower cultural and food day.

**Day 5 — A final morning, then onward**  
Take breakfast, tea or a park walk before your driver brings you to the airport or station.

### Who this route suits

Families, multi-generation groups, older travellers, food-minded couples and first-time visitors wanting a softer chapter between major cities.

### Practical notes

The panda base requires an early start and meaningful walking. Summer is hot and humid. Leshan involves driving and optional steps; it is not necessary for the route to feel complete. Panda activity cannot be guaranteed, though mornings are generally preferable.
