# Gardens, Water Towns & Tea — Shanghai, Suzhou & Hangzhou

**One-line summary:** A 6-day, 5-night Jiangnan journey of classical gardens, canals and tea country, designed as a quiet extension rather than a sequence of crowded day trips.

## Research summary (Step 1)

### 市场地位与证据

上海—苏州—杭州是Western market最成熟的中国东部短线之一。Audley、Wendy Wu、China Highlights、WildChina、A&K式私人产品和TourRadar长期销售；同里、南浔、乌镇或周庄通常作为一个water town加入。其优势是国际航班、高铁便利、较低海拔和清晰的园林/茶/运河叙事。

### 常见时长与顺序

- 常见时长：5–7天。
- 上海2晚 → 苏州1–2晚 → 水乡半天或一晚 → 杭州2晚。
- 紧凑版常把苏州作为上海一日游，把杭州压缩到西湖与茶园一天。

### 市场通常包含

高铁或车辆、英文导游、园林门票、运河船、西湖和龙井茶村。普通版本容易重复多个相似园林和古镇，并在中午最拥挤时抵达。

### 常见营销对象

情侣、60+旅客、设计与园林兴趣者、短途访华者、游轮前后延伸和不希望增加国内航班的人。

## Comparative analysis (Step 2)

### Typical version

上海出发当天往返苏州，快速看两个园林、运河和丝绸购物；再用另一天往返杭州。水乡被当作拍照停靠，没有清晨或傍晚的生活气息。

### Our version

- 苏州至少住一晚，只选择一个最适合客人兴趣和体力的主要园林。
- 水乡只选一个，不用周庄、乌镇、同里连续复制相似体验。
- 私人车在苏州—水乡—杭州段处理行李和门到门交通。
- 杭州安排完整茶村半天，饮茶和午餐比购物更重要。
- 每天允许较晚开始或较早结束，适合长者和跨洲航班后的恢复。

## Client-facing copy (Step 3)

### Overview

Jiangnan is not a list of water towns. It is a cultivated way of seeing: a borrowed view through a garden window, a canal turning behind whitewashed walls, tea terraces held in morning mist. Staying overnight changes the experience, allowing the region to feel lived in after the day-trip traffic has gone.

### Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive in Shanghai**  
Private airport transfer and a light first evening near the river or your hotel neighbourhood.

**Day 2 — Shanghai as a port city**  
Connect the Bund’s architecture with a lived-in district, food and contemporary design. The route remains private and shopping-free unless requested.

**Day 3 — Suzhou, one garden properly seen**  
Travel by high-speed rail or private car. Your guide uses one exceptional garden to explain space, poetry and architecture, then follows a quieter canal lane. Stay after the day-trippers leave.

**Day 4 — A water town, then Hangzhou**  
Choose Tongli, Nanxun or another town according to route and crowd conditions. Walk a manageable canal section, have lunch and continue privately to Hangzhou.

**Day 5 — West Lake and Longjing tea country**  
See the lake at its gentler hour, then continue to a tea village for landscape, processing and a meal. There is no requirement to purchase tea.

**Day 6 — Departure or onward travel**  
Transfer to Hangzhou airport or return to Shanghai according to the next flight or journey.

### Who this route suits

Older travellers, couples, garden and design lovers, short-stay visitors and anyone seeking a calm prelude or finale to a longer China trip.

### Practical notes

Spring and autumn are comfortable; plum-rain season is humid and wet. Garden and old-town paving can be uneven. Popular water towns are crowded on weekends and public holidays, so town choice and timing remain flexible.
